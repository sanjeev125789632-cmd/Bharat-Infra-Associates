Track Record / 02

A hundred projects on, and still counting.

About Us / 04

A contractor built around its own crews.

Solar, telecom and construction — under one accountable team.

Bharat Infra Associates is a service provider company engaged in the Solar, Telecom and Construction sectors, established in 2021 and headed by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan.

We deliver all types of solar projects, civil works, erection, AC/DC electrical works, communication networks and telecom infrastructure — making it easier for clients to execute projects efficiently across India. With a commitment to excellence and a planning-, execution- and quality-focused approach, we have completed over 100 projects, primarily across Bihar and Jharkhand, and continue to expand operations nationwide.

Our business approach is based on collaboration, transparency and delivering true value for money, supported by a direct and experienced local workforce that controls quality and timely delivery — on work ranging from straightforward extensions to complex conversion and refurbishment projects.

BK Mr. Bundan Khan Managing Director
AY Mr. Ayan Surveyor Team Head

Supported by a dedicated Financial Advisor and Project Manager overseeing commercial control and site delivery.

Make execution the easy part for our clients.

Our mission is to make project delivery straightforward — absorbing the coordination across solar, civil, electrical and telecom scopes so clients deal with one team, one programme and one standard of workmanship.

We hold to a planning-, execution- and quality-focused method: scope agreed before mobilisation, crews resourced directly rather than sub-let, and progress reported openly. Transparency is not a courtesy here — it is what keeps a programme honest.

And we measure value the way a client does: work that is right the first time, handed over on the date committed, at a price that reflects true value for money.

From the eastern corridor to a national footprint.

Our vision is to grow from a proven regional contractor into a national infrastructure partner — carrying the same crew-led model that earned our record in Bihar and Jharkhand into every state we enter.

We see India's energy and communications build-out as a long programme, not a cycle. Solar capacity, fibre reach and tower density will keep rising, and the contractors who last will be the ones who can be relied on for the unglamorous parts: foundations that hold, terminations that pass, dates that stand.

That is the company we are building — deeper capability, wider geography, and the same answer when a client asks who is accountable.

Culture / 05

How we work, when nobody is watching the site.

Three commitments that shape how our crews are hired, briefed and held to account.

Principle 01

Collaboration

We work alongside the client's team rather than behind a contract boundary — shared programmes, joint site walks, and problems raised early enough to still be cheap.

Principle 02

Transparency

Progress, costs and constraints are reported as they are. A slipped date reported on Monday is a schedule issue; the same date discovered at handover is a dispute.

Principle 03

True value for money

A direct, experienced local workforce keeps quality and timing in our own hands. No margin stacked through layers of sub-contract, and no surprises at final account.
