Erection Services

Bharat Infra Associates lists structural and mechanical erection among its infrastructure services. The company was founded in 2021, is led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan and has project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand. The published scope includes tower erection, structure lifting, alignment, torque control, grouting and final tightening. This page does not claim lifting capacities, crew numbers, safety certifications or project results that have not been provided.

Work covered

An erection enquiry should identify the structure type, drawings, component schedule, lifting plan responsibility, access and working area. The scope may concern lattice or monopole towers, solar mounting structures or other listed structural work. Assembly sequence, setting-out information, bolt grades, torque requirements, alignment tolerances and grouting details must come from the approved documents. Bharat Infra Associates does not publish standard lifting limits or equipment lists, so the proposed method and resources need to be reviewed against the actual site requirement.

  • Lattice and monopole tower erection
  • Solar module mounting structure assembly
  • Heavy-structure lifting and alignment
  • Torque control, grouting and final tightening
  • Height work under a documented project method

Information required for an enquiry

For a useful scope review, send the project location, the required service, a brief description of the work and the expected start period. Include approved drawings, quantities or a bill of quantities when available. State which materials and equipment are client-supplied, which activities are included in the contractor package and which interfaces belong to other parties. Access restrictions, working hours, storage arrangements and any live-site conditions should be identified before programme or price is discussed.

Testing and handover

Inspection and handover should cover only the agreed erection package. Relevant records may include component checks, alignment readings, connection completion, specified torque records, grouting status and snag closure when required by the project documents. Height-work arrangements and access restrictions must be discussed before mobilisation. The client should identify permit systems, interface responsibilities and witnessing requirements. This avoids presenting a general service description as proof of a particular method, certification or completed project.

Scope boundaries

Final inclusions and exclusions depend on the documents issued for enquiry and the agreed commercial offer. Project capacity, quantities, dates, mobilization periods and outcomes are not stated on this page because verified values have not been supplied. Any additional design responsibility, procurement, statutory approval, specialist testing or long-term maintenance requirement should be written into the enquiry rather than assumed from the general service name.

Scope review before mobilisation

Before a final scope is issued, the parties should review drawings, quantities, site access, material responsibility, work interfaces, inspection points and required records together. Any change after agreement should be documented against the relevant work package. This review keeps the website’s general service description separate from the technical and commercial obligations accepted for an individual project.
