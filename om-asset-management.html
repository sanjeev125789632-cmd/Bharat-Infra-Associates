Asset Management and O&M

Bharat Infra Associate lists asset management and operations and maintenance as a post-handover service area. The stated scope includes preventive and breakdown maintenance, module cleaning, thermography, string checks, spares management, fault rectification and performance reporting. No availability percentage, response time or energy-gain claim is published here.

Work covered

An O&M enquiry should identify the plant or asset type, location, equipment schedule, available drawings, operating history and present condition. The client should state whether the requirement is for a planned maintenance schedule, breakdown attendance, module cleaning, thermography, electrical string checks, spares coordination or a combined package. Site access, shutdown rules, reporting frequency and material responsibility also need to be clear. The website does not publish a standard service level or response period, so those terms must be agreed for the specific requirement.

  • Preventive and breakdown maintenance schedules
  • Solar-module cleaning and visual site checks
  • Thermography and string checks
  • Spares coordination and fault rectification

Information needed for an O&M enquiry

An O&M scope depends on the asset as it stands today. Send the equipment schedule, the available drawings, the operating history and a note on present condition. No standard service level, visit frequency or response period is published, so those terms have to be agreed for the specific requirement.

  • Asset type, location, installed capacity where known, and commissioning date
  • Maintenance frequency wanted: visit interval for preventive work, and expected response for breakdowns
  • Module cleaning: frequency, water source and who supplies water, and access arrangements
  • Inspection scope: what is walked, measured or opened at each visit
  • Fault reporting route: who is told, in what format, and within what period
  • Spares: held by client, procured against approval, or drawn from OEM warranty stock

Thermography and string checks

Thermography and electrical string checks are listed activities, and either can be included where the contract says so. Both need a defined method before they are useful: the inspection interval, the irradiance or load condition under which readings are taken, the equipment used, the reporting format, and the threshold that counts as a defect. State who acts on a defect once it is reported.

Maintenance reports and spare-parts responsibility

Each maintenance activity should close with a record of the work performed, observations, and any action left with another party. Agree the reporting period and format at the start. Fault rectification can depend on spare availability, OEM instructions and authority to isolate or energise equipment, so the enquiry should say who holds each of those. No equipment warranty, availability percentage or energy-gain result is implied by this service description.

Reporting basis and data

Performance reporting requires an agreed source of operational data and a clear contracted baseline. Thermography and string checks also require a defined inspection method, safe access and an agreed output format. Fault rectification may depend on spare availability, OEM instructions and authorization to isolate or energise equipment. The handover from each maintenance activity should record the work performed, observations and any pending action required by another party. No equipment warranty or performance result is implied by this service description.

Frequently asked questions

What does an operations and maintenance enquiry need to state?

Give the asset type, location, equipment schedule and present condition, and say whether you need planned maintenance, breakdown attendance, module cleaning, thermography, string checks, spares coordination or a combined package. Also state water availability for cleaning, shutdown rules, site access and reporting frequency. No standard service level or response period is published.

Who supplies spare parts under an O&M contract?

That is agreed per contract and is not assumed here. The enquiry should say whether spares are held by the client, procured by the contractor against approval, or drawn from OEM warranty stock. Fault rectification can also depend on OEM instructions and on who holds authority to isolate and energise the equipment.
