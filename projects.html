Geographic experience

Project experience is stated for two states: Bihar and Jharkhand. The head office is in Delhi, which is where enquiries are received and reviewed — it is not itself a location of project work. No claim is made to experience in every district of either state, and no district, city, project name, capacity or completion date is published, because verified records for those have not been supplied.

This page replaced separate Bihar and Jharkhand pages on 22 September 2026. Those two pages carried the same general service text with only the state name changed, and neither held district-level evidence, an approved project summary, original site photographs, a completion period or any other state-specific fact. Publishing two near-identical location pages adds nothing a buyer can act on, so their content was consolidated here and both URLs now redirect to this page.

What a state-level enquiry should state

Give the district and nearest town, the site type and access conditions, the service required and the expected start period. Add drawings, schedules, quantities and the responsibility matrix where they exist. Identify client-supplied materials, the interfaces with other contractors, the inspection points, the tests required and the handover records expected. Access, storage, utilities, working hours and permits are reviewed against the actual site rather than assumed from the state.

Services available for enquiries in either state

The same six service scopes apply in Bihar and Jharkhand: solar EPC, telecom infrastructure, civil works, erection services, AC/DC electrical works and asset management and O&M. Each of those pages sets out the information needed before that scope can be reviewed. A single enquiry may combine several of them, in which case name the boundary between each package.

What a published project record will contain

No project record is published yet. When Bharat Infra Associate supplies approved details, each entry will state the project type, the state and district, whether the work was direct or subcontracted, the contracted scope, a measurable quantity, the start and completion period, the work actually performed, the inspection and testing records produced, and the handover scope — with original photographs captioned to what they show. Anything that cannot be evidenced will be left out rather than estimated.
