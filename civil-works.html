Civil Works

Civil works are one of the service areas listed by Bharat Infra Associates. Founded in 2021 and led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan, the company has project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand and a head office in Delhi. This page describes the civil activities already stated in the company’s capability information. It does not add unverified project quantities, concrete volumes, locations, clients, certifications or completion outcomes.

Work covered

An infrastructure civil package begins with approved drawings, site levels, quantities and clear responsibility for materials, plant and disposal. The listed scope includes excavation, PCC and RCC foundations, pedestals and equipment plinths. It also includes trenches and cable ducts, boundary work, roads, drainage and site levelling where specified. Conversion or refurbishment work requires an additional record of existing conditions. The website does not state standard foundation types or quantities, so the design and bill of quantities supplied for the enquiry remain the basis of review.

  • Excavation, PCC and RCC foundations
  • Pedestals and equipment plinths
  • Trenching and cable ducting
  • Boundary walls, internal roads and drainage
  • Site levelling, conversion and refurbishment work

Information required for an enquiry

For a useful scope review, send the project location, the required service, a brief description of the work and the expected start period. Include approved drawings, quantities or a bill of quantities when available. State which materials and equipment are client-supplied, which activities are included in the contractor package and which interfaces belong to other parties. Access restrictions, working hours, storage arrangements and any live-site conditions should be identified before programme or price is discussed.

Testing and handover

Civil inspection and handover must follow the documents agreed for the project. The enquiry should identify required checks for excavation levels, reinforcement, formwork, concrete placement, embedded items, plinth positions, trench routes, drainage falls and finished levels. Where another contractor supplies equipment or structures, interface points and tolerances should be clear before work begins. Any laboratory testing, third-party inspection or statutory approval must be identified by the client because no blanket certification or test regime is claimed on this website.

Scope boundaries

Final inclusions and exclusions depend on the documents issued for enquiry and the agreed commercial offer. Project capacity, quantities, dates, mobilization periods and outcomes are not stated on this page because verified values have not been supplied. Any additional design responsibility, procurement, statutory approval, specialist testing or long-term maintenance requirement should be written into the enquiry rather than assumed from the general service name.

Scope review before mobilisation

Before a final scope is issued, the parties should review drawings, quantities, site access, material responsibility, work interfaces, inspection points and required records together. Any change after agreement should be documented against the relevant work package. This review keeps the website’s general service description separate from the technical and commercial obligations accepted for an individual project.
