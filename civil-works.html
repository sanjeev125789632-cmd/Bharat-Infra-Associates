Work covered

An infrastructure civil package begins with approved drawings, site levels, quantities and clear responsibility for materials, plant and disposal. The listed scope includes excavation, PCC and RCC foundations, pedestals and equipment plinths. It also includes trenches and cable ducts, boundary work, roads, drainage and site levelling where specified. Conversion or refurbishment work requires an additional record of existing conditions. The website does not state standard foundation types or quantities, so the design and bill of quantities supplied for the enquiry remain the basis of review.

  • Excavation, PCC and RCC foundations
  • Pedestals and equipment plinths
  • Trenching and cable ducting
  • Boundary walls, internal roads and drainage
  • Site levelling, conversion and refurbishment work

Information needed for a civil package

A civil enquiry is reviewed against drawings, levels and quantities. Send the approved general arrangement and foundation drawings with their revision number, the site levels or a topographical survey where one exists, and the bill of quantities. Where quantities are not yet issued, say so rather than estimating them.

  • Excavation: depths, soil type if known, dewatering need, and who removes spoil
  • PCC and RCC: concrete grades, reinforcement schedules and who supplies the mix
  • Foundations and plinths: type, setting-out datum and bolt or insert positions
  • Trenches and drainage: routes, invert levels, falls and backfill specification
  • Roads and access works: formation width, sub-base specification and finished level
  • Boundary work and site levelling, where included

Stages and inspection hold points

Civil work is accepted stage by stage, so the enquiry should name the hold points rather than leave them to be argued later. Typical stages are excavation to level, blinding or PCC, reinforcement fixing before pour, formwork, embedded item position, concrete placement and curing, then backfill and finished level. Trench and drainage work adds invert level and falls; road work adds subgrade and sub-base acceptance.

Material, plant and disposal responsibility

State who supplies cement, aggregate, steel, shuttering and water, whether ready-mix or site-mix concrete is intended, who provides plant, and who is responsible for spoil disposal and for reinstating areas used for storage. Conversion or refurbishment work also needs a recorded survey of existing conditions before it starts.

Inspection and acceptance

Civil inspection and handover must follow the documents agreed for the project. The enquiry should identify required checks for excavation levels, reinforcement, formwork, concrete placement, embedded items, plinth positions, trench routes, drainage falls and finished levels. Where another contractor supplies equipment or structures, interface points and tolerances should be clear before work begins. Any laboratory testing, third-party inspection or statutory approval must be identified by the client because no blanket certification or test regime is claimed on this website.

Scope boundaries

Final inclusions and exclusions come from the documents issued for enquiry and the agreed commercial offer, not from this page. Capacities, quantities, dates and outcomes are not published here because verified values have not been supplied.

Frequently asked questions

Does Bharat Infra Associate undertake civil-only work packages?

Yes. Civil works is a listed service area in its own right, covering excavation, PCC and RCC foundations, pedestals and equipment plinths, trenches and cable ducts, boundary walls, roads, drainage and site levelling. Whether a particular enquiry stays civil-only or forms part of a combined package is fixed by the approved scope, drawings and bill of quantities.

Which inspection stages apply to a civil package?

The stages come from the project's own inspection plan, not from this page. Most enquiries need hold points named for excavation to level, reinforcement before pour, formwork, embedded item position and finished level, plus invert levels and falls on trench and drainage work. State who witnesses each stage and who arranges laboratory testing.
