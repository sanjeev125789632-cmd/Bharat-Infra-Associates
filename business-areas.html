Business areas and service scope

Service information is separated by work type so procurement and project teams can review the relevant scope directly.

Service overview

Bharat Infra Associates provides solar EPC, telecom infrastructure, civil works, erection, AC/DC electrical works, asset management and operations and maintenance services. The company was founded in 2021 and has stated project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand.

Solar EPC

Ground-mount and rooftop work may include foundations, module mounting structures, module installation, string layouts, inverter and transformer yard work, AC/DC cabling, testing and commissioning where specified.

Telecom infrastructure

The stated scope includes tower civil activity, lattice and monopole tower erection, antenna mounting, optical-fibre trenching, ducting, blowing, splicing, network integration, upgrades, swaps and extensions.

Civil works

Civil work may include excavation, PCC and RCC foundations, pedestals, equipment plinths, trenches, cable ducts, boundary walls, roads, drainage and site levelling.

Erection services

Structural and mechanical erection may include lifting, alignment, torque control, grouting and final tightening according to approved project documents.

AC/DC electrical works

The stated electrical scope includes DC string cabling, combiner boxes, surge-protection devices, AC LT panels, cables, glanding, terminations, earthing and lightning protection.

Asset management and O&M

Requirements may include preventive and breakdown maintenance, module cleaning, thermography, string checks, spares coordination, fault rectification and performance reporting on an agreed basis.

Define the work package

Each service page explains the stated work, typical interfaces, checks and enquiry information. Final responsibilities must be confirmed against drawings, quantities, specifications, material responsibility, inspection requirements and handover records for the individual project.
