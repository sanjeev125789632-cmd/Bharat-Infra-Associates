AC/DC Electrical Works

AC/DC electrical work is part of the service scope published by Bharat Infra Associate. Founded in 2021 and led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan, the company has project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand. The stated activities include solar DC strings, combiner boxes, surge-protection devices, AC LT panels, cabling, glanding, terminations, earthing and lightning protection. No voltage range, equipment brand, certification or test result is added beyond that supplied scope.

Work covered

An electrical enquiry should provide the approved single-line diagrams, layouts, cable schedule, termination details, equipment interfaces and material responsibility. DC work can include string routes, cables and combiner-box connections where specified. AC work can include LT panels, cable laying, glanding and termination. Earthing and lightning-protection requirements must be defined by the project design. The website does not state standard voltage, current or fault-level limits, so the supplied technical documents are required before a scope can be evaluated.

  • Combiner boxes and surge-protection devices
  • AC LT panels, cabling, glanding and terminations
  • Earthing and lightning-protection work
  • Continuity, insulation-resistance, polarity and pre-charge checks

Information needed for an AC/DC electrical package

Electrical work is quoted against the design, not against a service name. Send the approved single-line diagram and layouts with their revision number, the cable schedule, the termination details and the earthing and lightning-protection design. No standard voltage, current or fault level is published on this website, so the supplied design governs.

  • Cable schedule: sizes, types, lengths, and from-to references for every run
  • Cable routes: trench, tray, conduit or buried, and who provides the route
  • Terminations: lug type, gland type, and who supplies glands, lugs and ferrules
  • DC side: string layout, combiner box positions and surge-protection requirement
  • AC side: LT panel schedule, incomer and outgoing details, and panel supplier
  • Earthing: electrode type, conductor size, and the lightning-protection arrangement

Inspection and test responsibilities

The published scope refers to continuity, insulation-resistance, polarity and pre-charge checks together with testing and commissioning. Which of these is in a given package is a contract question. The project must name the instruments, the test format, the acceptance criteria, the witnessing party, and who holds authority to isolate and energise. Connections into client or OEM equipment need written interface approval first.

Handover records

Define the completion pack before work starts: cable schedule marked as installed, test sheets for each circuit tested, earthing readings, panel and termination photographs where required, and a list of any circuit left un-energised and why. This page does not imply a statutory authorisation, an electrical licence or an equipment-manufacturer approval; supply separate evidence if a project requires one.
