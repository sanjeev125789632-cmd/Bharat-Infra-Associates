AC/DC Electrical Works

AC/DC electrical work is part of the service scope published by Bharat Infra Associates. Founded in 2021 and led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan, the company has project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand. The stated activities include solar DC strings, combiner boxes, surge-protection devices, AC LT panels, cabling, glanding, terminations, earthing and lightning protection. No voltage range, equipment brand, certification or test result is added beyond that supplied scope.

Work covered

An electrical enquiry should provide the approved single-line diagrams, layouts, cable schedule, termination details, equipment interfaces and material responsibility. DC work can include string routes, cables and combiner-box connections where specified. AC work can include LT panels, cable laying, glanding and termination. Earthing and lightning-protection requirements must be defined by the project design. The website does not state standard voltage, current or fault-level limits, so the supplied technical documents are required before a scope can be evaluated.

  • DC string cabling and string layout
  • Combiner boxes and surge-protection devices
  • AC LT panels, cabling, glanding and terminations
  • Earthing and lightning-protection work
  • Continuity, insulation-resistance, polarity and pre-charge checks

Information required for an enquiry

For a useful scope review, send the project location, the required service, a brief description of the work and the expected start period. Include approved drawings, quantities or a bill of quantities when available. State which materials and equipment are client-supplied, which activities are included in the contractor package and which interfaces belong to other parties. Access restrictions, working hours, storage arrangements and any live-site conditions should be identified before programme or price is discussed.

Testing and handover

The existing capability information refers to continuity, insulation-resistance, polarity and pre-charge checks, as well as testing and commissioning. The project must define the instruments, formats, acceptance criteria, witnessing party and responsibility for energisation. Connections to client or OEM equipment require clear interface approval. Handover should identify the records required for the contracted package. This page does not imply statutory authorization, a particular electrical licence or approval from an equipment manufacturer unless separate evidence is supplied.

Scope boundaries

Final inclusions and exclusions depend on the documents issued for enquiry and the agreed commercial offer. Project capacity, quantities, dates, mobilization periods and outcomes are not stated on this page because verified values have not been supplied. Any additional design responsibility, procurement, statutory approval, specialist testing or long-term maintenance requirement should be written into the enquiry rather than assumed from the general service name.

Scope review before mobilisation

Before a final scope is issued, the parties should review drawings, quantities, site access, material responsibility, work interfaces, inspection points and required records together. Any change after agreement should be documented against the relevant work package. This review keeps the website’s general service description separate from the technical and commercial obligations accepted for an individual project.
