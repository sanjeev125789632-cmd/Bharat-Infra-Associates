About Bharat Infra Associates

Learn about Bharat Infra Associates, founded in 2021 and led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan, serving solar, telecom, civil and electrical scopes.

Company background

Bharat Infra Associates was founded in 2021. The company is led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan and has its head office at B-49, 3rd Floor, Mehtab Complex, Joshi Colony, IP Extension, Delhi 110092, India. Its stated project experience is in Bihar and Jharkhand. This page records only those supplied facts and the service descriptions already published on the website.

The company’s capability profile covers solar EPC, telecom infrastructure, civil works, structural and mechanical erection, AC/DC electrical works, asset management and operations and maintenance. These service areas may form separate packages or related parts of an infrastructure requirement. The exact work included in any enquiry depends on approved drawings, quantities, location, access, material responsibility, testing requirements and the agreed commercial scope.

Services

Solar EPC work is described as ground-mount and rooftop plant activity, including civil foundations, module mounting structures, module installation, string layouts, inverter and transformer yard work, AC/DC cabling, testing and commissioning where specified. The website does not state project capacities or completed-project numbers. Those details should only be added when Bharat Infra Associates supplies approved supporting information.

Telecom infrastructure work includes tower civil activity, lattice and monopole tower erection, antenna mounting, optical-fibre trenching, ducting, blowing and splicing, network build and integration, site upgrades, swaps and extensions. Civil services include excavation, PCC and RCC foundations, pedestals, equipment plinths, trenches, cable ducts, boundary walls, roads, drainage and site levelling. Erection work includes lifting, alignment, torque control, grouting and final tightening according to the project documents.

AC/DC electrical work includes DC string cabling, combiner boxes, surge-protection devices, AC LT panels, cables, glanding, terminations, earthing and lightning protection. The stated checks include continuity, insulation resistance, polarity and pre-charge checks. Asset management and O&M can include preventive and breakdown maintenance, module cleaning, thermography, string checks, spares coordination, fault rectification and performance reporting against an agreed contractual basis.

How to prepare an enquiry

A useful enquiry should include the company name, contact details, project location, required service, brief scope and expected start period. Where available, include drawings, schedules, bills of quantities and the responsibility matrix. Identify client-supplied materials, interfaces with other contractors, required tests and handover documents. Do not send confidential documents through an unsecured channel. Commercial commitments, project dates and technical acceptance should be based on the formal documents exchanged for the specific requirement.
