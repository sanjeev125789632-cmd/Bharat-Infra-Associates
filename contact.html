Frequently asked questions

How are testing and handover responsibilities agreed?

They are written into the contract documents, not assumed from a service description. The enquiry should name the tests required, the format and acceptance criteria for each, the witnessing party, and the records handed over at completion. Where another contractor supplies equipment or structures, fix the interface and tolerance before work starts.

What should a first enquiry contain?

The project location, the service required, a short description of the work and the expected start period. Add drawings, quantities and the responsibility matrix where they exist, and say which materials are client-supplied. Do not send confidential drawings, identity documents or banking details through the public form.
