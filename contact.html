A clear first enquiry helps the company understand what is required without assuming details that have not been agreed. State the project location, the service needed, the broad work scope and the expected start period. If a requirement combines civil, erection and electrical packages, identify the boundaries between them and the work expected from other contractors.

Information to include

Where available, identify the project type, site access conditions, drawing status, approximate quantities and material responsibility. Note whether testing, commissioning, handover documents or O&M support are part of the request. These details are used to start a discussion; they do not replace approved drawings, a bill of quantities, a responsibility matrix or a formal commercial agreement.

Do not submit confidential drawings, identity documents, banking information or other sensitive files through this public form. The form collects only the contact and scope details needed for an initial response. Protected or commercially sensitive documents should be exchanged later through an agreed secure channel.
