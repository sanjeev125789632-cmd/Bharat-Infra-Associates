Telecom Infrastructure

Bharat Infra Associates provides telecom infrastructure services as part of its stated capability profile. The company was founded in 2021, is led by Managing Director Mr. Bundan Khan and has project experience in Bihar and Jharkhand. The service description covers tower-related civil and erection work, antenna mounting, optical-fibre routes and communication-network activities. No tower counts, operator names, network results or completed-site statistics are claimed on this page.

Work covered

A telecom enquiry should identify the site type, access conditions, tower or network package, approved drawings and responsibility split. Tower work may involve civil preparation, foundations, erection and antenna mounting where those items are included in the agreed scope. Fibre work may include trenching, ducts, cable blowing and splicing. Network build and integration requirements must identify the equipment, interfaces, testing method and party responsible for acceptance. The website does not publish standard tower heights, fibre lengths or crew quantities, so those details must be provided for review.

  • Tower civil work and foundation scope
  • Lattice and monopole tower erection
  • Antenna mounting and associated site work
  • OFC trenching, ducting, blowing and splicing
  • Network build, integration, upgrades, swaps and extensions

Information required for an enquiry

For a useful scope review, send the project location, the required service, a brief description of the work and the expected start period. Include approved drawings, quantities or a bill of quantities when available. State which materials and equipment are client-supplied, which activities are included in the contractor package and which interfaces belong to other parties. Access restrictions, working hours, storage arrangements and any live-site conditions should be identified before programme or price is discussed.

Testing and handover

Inspection and handover requirements vary between civil, tower and fibre packages. The enquiry should state the required records for foundation work, erection alignment, bolted connections, cable routes, splicing and network integration. Where upgrades, swaps or capacity extensions are involved, the existing site condition and any live-network restrictions should be documented. Bharat Infra Associates can then review the requested work against the location and programme without implying an operator relationship, equipment approval or technical standard that has not been supplied.

Scope boundaries

Final inclusions and exclusions depend on the documents issued for enquiry and the agreed commercial offer. Project capacity, quantities, dates, mobilization periods and outcomes are not stated on this page because verified values have not been supplied. Any additional design responsibility, procurement, statutory approval, specialist testing or long-term maintenance requirement should be written into the enquiry rather than assumed from the general service name.

Scope review before mobilisation

Before a final scope is issued, the parties should review drawings, quantities, site access, material responsibility, work interfaces, inspection points and required records together. Any change after agreement should be documented against the relevant work package. This review keeps the website’s general service description separate from the technical and commercial obligations accepted for an individual project.
